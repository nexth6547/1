Lunar color and elevation maps

Source: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio, CGI Moon Kit (ID 4720)
https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/

Color map: lroc_color_2k.jpg
Elevation map: ldem_3_8bit.jpg

The maps are derived from Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Camera (LROC) and Lunar
Orbiter Laser Altimeter (LOLA) data. NASA SVS requests credit to NASA's
Scientific Visualization Studio.

Saturn and Neptune intro textures

Source imagery:
- Neptune: NASA/JPL, Voyager 2 false-colour image PIA00057
  https://images.nasa.gov/details/PIA00057

Saturn uses a seamless 2048x1024 procedural equirectangular atmosphere map;
its bands are artistic rather than source imagery. The Saturn ring map is a
local polar texture strip with radial bands. Neptune's local colour and bump
maps are extended over the unseen hemisphere for spherical mapping. No network
access is needed at runtime.
